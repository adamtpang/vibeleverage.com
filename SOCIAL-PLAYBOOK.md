# The social capital playbook, as a game

Companion to SOCIAL-LEVERAGE.md. The eleven moves that build social capital, scored as skills you level up with real evidence. This file holds the rules only. Personal scorecards stay private.

## The eleven skills

| # | Skill | What it looks like | Evidence that counts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Give first | Useful intros, gifts, feedback, attention before any ask | A thing given with no ask attached |
| 2 | Follow through | Do what you said, close loops, reply | A promise kept, a loop closed, dated |
| 3 | Make things together | Episodes, projects, collaborations | A shipped thing with another person's name on it |
| 4 | Bridge groups | Introduce people from worlds that do not meet | An introduction that led to a real conversation |
| 5 | Show up repeatedly | Recurring contact, in person where possible | Second, third and fourth contact with the same person |
| 6 | Public body of work | Work strangers can find | Published episodes, posts, repos |
| 7 | Join small groups | Clubs, communities, scenes | Regular attendance, not a signup |
| 8 | Host | Dinners, calls, groups you run | An event you organized that happened |
| 9 | Ask small favors | The Ben Franklin effect | A small ask made and granted |
| 10 | Credit and amplify | Share and praise others publicly | A public shoutout with a link |
| 11 | Be reliable and honest | Kept promises, honest numbers | No broken promise open longer than two weeks |

## Levels

Each skill has a level from 0 to 5. A level needs dated evidence, never intent or plans.

| Level | Name | Bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Dormant | No evidence in the last 90 days |
| 1 | Spark | One real instance |
| 2 | Habit | Three or more instances across a month |
| 3 | Practice | Weekly for a month |
| 4 | Craft | Weekly for three months, with a result someone else can see |
| 5 | Signature | Other people describe you by it |

## XP

| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Something given with no ask attached | 10 |
| A loop closed that someone was waiting on | 15 |
| A thing shipped with another person | 30 |
| An introduction that led to a real conversation | 25 |
| A second conversation with the same person | 20 |
| A public piece of work published | 15 |
| An event hosted that happened | 30 |
| A public credit or shoutout | 5 |
| Help that came back unasked | 40, the best signal in the game |

Planning, drafting, research and tooling earn zero XP. Only contact with people and shipped work count.

## Debuffs

Open debts drain the game. Each one halves the XP earned from new asks until it is cleared.

- **Waiting debt:** someone gave you time or work and is waiting on your reply or release for more than two weeks.
- **Broken promise:** a stated commitment past its date with no update to the person.
- **Ghosted loop:** a thread you started and abandoned.

Clear debts before chasing new relationships. Repaying an old tie is worth more than opening a new one.

## Weekly ritual, 15 minutes

1. Clear one debt.
2. Give one thing with no ask.
3. Log XP with dates.
4. Re-score any skill whose evidence changed.

## Boss

**Help that comes back unasked.** When someone you helped first opens a door for you without being asked, the game is working.
